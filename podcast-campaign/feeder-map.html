Modern Wisdom

Avery's path

Avery needs a thesis-led hook that fits MW's psychology, status and success lens. A good candidate is 'the psychology of status, taste and money in the blue-chip art world' (how hype is manufactured, why billionaires buy art), not just a founder biography. Step 1: land 4-6 mid-tier entrepreneur and lifestyle shows where his story fits naturally (School of Greatness, The Skinny Confidential Him & Her, Hot Smart Rich, and the guest's own podcasting peers) to build clips and a guest reel. Step 2: use those clips to pitch Jordan Harbinger (insider-world and persuasion angle) and Mel Robbins or Jay Shetty (purpose and philanthropy angle), the shows that precede MW most often in this sample. Step 3: get on shows whose hosts are in Chris Williamson's circle (Codie Sanchez's BigDeal, James Smith's The Problem With, DOAC) and pitch MW directly with a short clip plus a status-psychology or art-market thesis, ideally tied to a book or major news event, since that matches the timing of most MW bookings.

How guests get here: There is no single feeder. Most non-celebrity MW guests arrive on a book cycle and do a burst of interviews on large general-interest shows (DOAC, Jordan Harbinger, Mel Robbins, Plain English, Rich Roll) in the weeks or months before MW, which points to publicist-driven tours. Modern Wisdom also recycles its own guests heavily (Joe Hudson, George Mack, Anomaly, Harrington, Freya India), and many guests or feeder hosts are podcasters themselves (Codie Sanchez, Connor Beaton, Donald Robertson, Spencer Greenberg, Johnny Chang, James Smith, Steven Bartlett, Konstantin Kisin, Mel Robbins). Peer-to-peer creator networking is therefore a major route. Viral YouTube origin stories (Johnny Chang via Soft White Underbelly, Pantano's Pursuit of Wonder) are a second route, and health founders reach MW through the JRE and Ben Greenfield circuit. No booking agency or network showed up repeatedly apart from Flightstory (DOAC); not verified.

The Shawn Ryan Show

Avery's path

Start with story-driven business shows where Avery fits naturally: The Jordan Harbinger Show, PBD Podcast and The Diary of a CEO, pitching the Alec Monopoly brand-building story and ArtLife's early move online. Add one tech or abundance show (Moonshots or American Optimist) for the early-tech-adoption angle, and one investigative or crime show (Julian Dorey, or Tucker if there's a real exposé) for an art-crime or provenance angle. Tie the run to a concrete news hook, such as a major sale, an ArtLife milestone, a celebrity collaboration or a conservation auction, because SRS books non-military guests around a fresh moment. Then pitch SRS directly on angles that match its audience: his work with the Miami Police 'Do the Right Thing' program, art-market crime and money laundering, and conservation philanthropy, with the earlier episodes as social proof. Look for a warm introduction through an SRS alumnus (e.g., a Miami or Florida-based guest such as Ryan Montgomery, whose company is in Boca Raton).

How guests get here: Non-military guests mostly reach SRS on the back of a fresh, newsworthy moment: a viral investigation (Shirley, Robinson, Schlep), a funding or tech milestone (Scholl, Adcock, Bry, Taylor), or a book or discovery. That moment gets picked up first by a large business or culture show (Tucker, PBD, JRE, DOAC, Moonshots), usually 1-10 months before SRS. Defense-relevant tech founders tend to arrive through a cluster of VC and founder shows (Logan Bartlett, StrictlyVC, Relentless, American Optimist, Moonshots), while faith and mystery guests come through Blurry Creatures and Tucker. SRS also books from its own network: repeat guests (Luna, Montgomery, Shirley), guests linked to earlier guests (Schlep and Montgomery on Roblox), and hosts of feeder shows (Tucker, Lonsdale, Ramsey).

Avery's path

Avery's best entry is the business and lifestyle lane, not comedy. Start with PBD Podcast and Full Send, where luxury, Miami deal-making and celebrity stories land naturally.
