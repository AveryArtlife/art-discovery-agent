Who you're booking

The dealer behind the brand.

Avery Andon is a veteran art dealer, entrepreneur and philanthropist best known for developing street artist Alec Monopoly into a global sensation, with sold-out exhibitions from New York to Morocco and major brand endorsements.

In 2014 Avery founded ArtLife, one of the first online-only blue-chip art galleries. He coined the term "Click & Mortar" for a strategy that integrates online and social media marketing with curated physical pop-ups. Countless galleries have since emulated the model.

An early champion of artists such as Hebru Brantley and Jammie Holmes, Avery has advised celebrities and corporations on art investment. He also hosts his own show, ArtLife with Avery Andon, which takes listeners backstage in the worlds of art and entertainment.

His philanthropy includes serving as Co-Chair for UCLA Mattel Children's Hospital and Goldie Hawn's MindUP, co-founding City Seats with the New York Yankees to benefit the Boys & Girls Clubs of America, and supporting WildAid, the Amazon Conservation Team, Miami Police's Do the Right Thing initiative and Amigos for Kids.
