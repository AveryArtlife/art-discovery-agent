Where a candidate on the brief could not be tied to any company (Dana White venture, Kelly Clarkson, Scott Disick), a negative-finding record is included so the gap is documented rather than silent.

## 2. Record highlights by segment

### 2.1 Peptide-direct: RUO vendors and peptide clinics
- **BioLongevity Labs** (Jay Campbell, Hunter Williams, Josh Felber - cofounders) is the only credible *celebrity-influencer-founded* research-peptide manufacturer identified. Its growth narrative (Forbes, Jul 2026) is explicitly "founder trust as the asset." Campbell also holds a long-standing affiliate relationship with Limitless Life Nootropics.
- **Fountain Life** (Tony Robbins, Peter Diamandis, Drs. Kapp and Hariri) offers peptide therapy and NAD+ inside $10.5K-$85K memberships; raised $18M in Aug 2025.
- **10X Health** (Gary Brecka, Grant Cardone) offered peptides through clinic consults; the Nov 2024 firing of Brecka and the settled 2025 lawsuits are the clearest key-person-risk case in the set.
- **Ways2Well** lists Joe Rogan as a client and its founder is a repeat JRE guest; AP/CNN (Nov 2025) used it as the archetype of influencer-driven unproven-peptide sales.
- **Love.Life** (John Mackey) is launching a peptide program.

### 2.2 GLP-1 telehealth
- **Ro / Serena Williams** (Aug 21, 2025): first A-list "celebrity patient ambassador" for branded GLP-1 telehealth; multi-year, national campaign, Super Bowl LX. Related-party note: husband Alexis Ohanian is a Ro investor and board member.
- **Noom / Rebel Wilson** (Sept 18, 2025): "Chief Wellness Ambassador" for "microdose GLP-1s."
- **Hims & Hers**: no celebrity in the controversial 2025 compounded-GLP-1 Super Bowl ad (650% traffic spike); Common narrated the 2026 spot.
- **WeightWatchers**: Oprah's 2015-2024 director/shareholder arc and Queen Latifah's 2025 menopause-program role bookend the company's pivot to GLP-1 prescribing.
- **Lemme GLP-1 Daily** (Kourtney Kardashian Barker): not a GLP-1 drug - a botanical capsule using "GLP-1" on-label; strong sales claims ($13M single TikTok Shop month, Nov 2025) alongside California and New York class actions.

### 2.3 Diagnostics and longevity platforms with celebrity cap tables
- **Function Health** (Mark Hyman cofounder/CMO): $53M Series A (a16z) with Damon, Efron, Hart, Pascal, Shetty, Griffin, Kaepernick, Embiid; $298M Series B at $2.5B (Nov 2025); Ezra MRI acquisition.
- **Tally Health** (David Sinclair): $10M seed (2023) with Legend, Teigen, Pascal, Rhimes, Hart, Roll, Cummings, Efron - the same celebrity longevity cohort recurs across Tally, Function and Superpower.
- **Superpower**: $30M Series A (Apr 2025) with Logan Paul, Steve Aoki, Vanessa Hudgens, Giannis.
- **Neko Health** (Daniel Ek): $700M Series C (Jul 2026) with Schiffer, Sharapova, Henry, Iovine, Zuckerberg/Chan.
- **Prenuvo**: Cindy Crawford investor; Kim Kardashian's unpaid Aug 2023 post is the single most-analyzed celebrity health post of the cycle.

### 2.4 Peptide-positioned consumer products
- **Rhode** (Hailey Bieber): Peptide Lip Treatment was one of three launch SKUs; $1B sale to e.l.f. (announced May 28, 2025; closed Aug 5, 2025); $20M media impact value in 48 hours.
- **Goop** (Gwyneth Paltrow): peptide serum is now the top seller; 2018 $145K claims settlement remains the reference enforcement case.
- **SKKN by Kim**: 3-peptide eye cream; brand folded June 2025 and is being relaunched under Skims in 2026.
- **Vital Proteins** (Jennifer Aniston, CCO): collagen peptides; Aniston's public comments on injectable peptides (AP, Nov 2025) blur the line between cosmetic-peptide products and peptide therapy.
- **Blueprint** (Bryan Johnson): peptide scalp serum; founder publicly considering shutting down the company after NYT reported losses.

## 3. Lessons

### 3.1 When celebrity involvement created measurable advantage
1. **Founder-owned, product-led, peptide-positioned: Rhode.** Fastest celebrity beauty brand to a $1B valuation; the peptide lip SKU was the launch hero and reportedly sold out within an hour; $20M MIV in 48h on the e.l.f. deal (Launchmetrics via WWD). The lesson is not "Hailey Bieber," it is *founder equity + a single hero SKU + a trend term ("peptide") the audience already searches for*.
2. **Investor-as-distribution: AG1 and Onnit.** AG1 reached a $1.2B valuation (Jan 2022) with a cap table of podcast hosts (Rogan, Huberman, Ferriss, Attia) who also read its ads; Onnit's Alpha Brain sold out three runs on Rogan alone and exited to Unilever (2021). Equity aligned the promoter for a decade, not a campaign.
3. **Cap-table halo for diagnostics: Function Health.** Celebrity investor names (Damon, Pascal, Hart, Efron) lead every headline; valuation moved from Series A to $2.5B within 12 months. Investors did not run ads; their names did the earned-media work.
4. **Trust-transfer at scale: Prenuvo / Kim Kardashian.** An unpaid post produced a demand surge cited in the company's $120M raise narrative and listed by Forbes (Aug 2025) among endorsements that "earned millions." It also produced the loudest physician backlash of any record.
5. **Category legitimization: Ro / Serena Williams.** Trade press (Healthcare Brew, MM+M) framed it as a structural shift in pharma-style advertising. Ro has not published lift metrics; treat impact as reputational/category rather than proven conversion.
6. **Sales claims that need audit: Lemme.** >$30M cumulative revenue in 16 months, #1 beauty ingestible at Target, $13M single TikTok Shop month (Nov 2025) - all company-sourced via case-study sites. Real, but arriving alongside two class actions.
7. **Public-market proof of dependence: Oprah / WeightWatchers.** Entry (2015) roughly doubled the stock; exit announcement (Feb 28, 2024) cut it ~25% in a day. This is the only record with a market-priced measurement of one celebrity's brand value.

### 3.2 When involvement appears cosmetic
- **Ladder (LeBron James, Arnold Schwarzenegger, Cindy Crawford, Lindsey Vonn):** ~$4M revenue after two years despite >180M combined followers; sold for an undisclosed sum to Openfit. Audience size without founder engagement or product differentiation does not convert.
- **SKKN by Kim:** the largest audience in the dataset (~360M) could not sustain a peptide-formulated skincare brand; folded in 2025.
- **Kin Euphorics / Bella Hadid:** "co-founder" title granted three years after founding; 2025 retail crowdfunding indicates limited scale.
- **Celebrity investor cohorts (Superpower, Neko, Tally):** press value at announcement; no evidence of continuing marketing activity.
- **Jennifer Aniston "Chief Creative Officer":** an executive title attached to a paid spokesperson role - useful for press, but not an operating role and not disclosed equity.

### 3.3 Founder-market fit
- Strong: Halle Berry (menopause, lived experience + legislative advocacy), Mark Hyman (functional-medicine practitioner), Peter Attia (physician, skeptical framing), Jay Campbell/Hunter Williams (the peptide audience *is* their audience), Gary Brecka (audience fit was excellent; governance was not).
- Weak or borrowed: Kardashian family "GLP-1" naming on a botanical; Daniel Ek (tech fame, not health credibility); Brooke Burke "Longevity" (name overlap only).
- Test for a peptide brand: does the celebrity already talk about the compound class without being paid (Rogan/BPC-157, Aniston/injectables, Disick/Mounjaro)? Those are the organic signals brands try to buy after the fact.

### 3.4 Trust transfer and audience conversion
- Medical-credibility transfer works fastest and breaks hardest: Prenuvo/Kardashian and 10X/Brecka both converted, then drew physician and academic (McGill OSS) rebuttals.
- Conversion follows *format*: podcast-host owners (AG1, Onnit, Momentous) convert because the recommendation is repeated weekly inside trusted long-form content; a single Super Bowl spot converts to traffic (Hims +650%) but not necessarily to durable brand trust.
- Testimonials attach to people, not brands: Dana White's transformation followed Brecka out of 10X Health.

### 3.5 Reputation and regulatory risk (cited cases)
- **Goop (2018):** $145,000 California DA settlement; injunction requiring "competent and reliable scientific evidence" for any efficacy claim - still the template for celebrity-wellness enforcement.
- **Kim Kardashian / SEC (Oct 2022):** $1.26M for failing to disclose a $250K payment despite an "#AD" tag - the analogy for paid peptide/GLP-1 endorsements: a hashtag is not full disclosure of the *amount and nature* of compensation when securities rules apply, and FTC "material connection" rules require clear, conspicuous disclosure in every post.
- **Lemme GLP-1 Daily (2025):** class actions in CA and NY over "GLP-1" naming and efficacy claims; critical coverage from Futurism, MDLinx, Healthline.
- **Liver King (2022-23):** hidden steroid/HGH use reframed as deceptive advertising; $25M class action (dismissed) - directly relevant to founders whose physique is the ad for a peptide brand.
- **Gary Brecka / 10X (2024-25):** pseudoscience critiques (McGill), credential questions, then founder-owner litigation.
- **Bryan Johnson / Blueprint (2025):** NYT reporting on losses and internal practices; founder says the company "hurts his credibility."
- **Hims & Hers (2025):** senator letters and FDA-adjacent criticism over compounded semaglutide ad risk disclosure.
- **Dr. Oz / iHerb (2025):** Senate letter alleging FTC-required disclosure was absent from product videos; forced divestiture on entering government.
- **Vital Proteins (2023):** recall of a product Aniston promoted - ambassadors inherit product-quality events.
- **Deepfake endorsements (Kelly Clarkson, Oprah):** fraudulent celebrity GLP-1/gummy ads degrade trust in legitimate campaigns and are an FTC enforcement priority.

### 3.6 Key-person risk and brand dependence
- **TB12** could not survive the Brady-Guerrero split; rebranded to TBRx with Brady as a "client."
- **10X Health** lost its face and immediately litigated over revenue tied to that face.
- **Blueprint** is inseparable from Bryan Johnson's body and reputation.
- **WeightWatchers** priced Oprah's exit at ~25% of market cap in a day.
- **Bulletproof** deliberately rebranded away from founder claims after Asprey's departure - a managed decoupling.
- Mitigants observed: earnouts (Rhode), operating cofounders with domain credentials (Function/Hyman + CEO; Lemme/Huck; Performance Inspired/Dowd), and cap-table rather than face-of-brand roles (Function, Neko).

### 3.7 Regulatory scrutiny specific to peptides (2025-26)
- HHS Secretary RFK Jr. announced (Feb 27, 2026) that ~14 of 19 Category-2 peptides move toward Category 1 effective Apr 23, 2026, with FDA PCAC review July 23-24, 2026. This is a *compounding eligibility* change, not approval; RUO vendors selling for human use remain exposed. Press (AP/CNN Nov 2025; PBS Apr 2026; Forbes Jun 2026) explicitly ties the policy to influencer demand (Rogan, Brecka, Means, Buhler), so any celebrity peptide launch will be read as MAHA-adjacent.
- Implication for a celebrity-fronted peptide brand: (1) decide up front whether the product is a compounded prescription (503A/503B, physician-gated), an RUO chemical (no human-use marketing, no influencer testimonials), or a cosmetic/collagen "peptide" (structure/function only); (2) treat the Kardashian SEC order and Goop injunction as the disclosure and substantiation floor; (3) contract for key-person events (earnout, morals and PED-disclosure clauses, testimonial ownership).
