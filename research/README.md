# Influencer Research — PackDraw & Mystery-Box / Case-Opening Brands

Competitive-intelligence database of creators who promote PackDraw and comparable
case-opening / mystery-box sites (HypeDrop, RillaBox, CSGORoll, DaddySkins, etc.),
built for marketing and partnership-outreach planning.

## Contents
- **[`REPORT.md`](./REPORT.md)** — narrative report: shortlist grouped by tier,
  synthesis of dominant creative patterns and funnel structure, and the shortlist
  re-clustered by creative format (dedicated-video / livestream / short-form /
  social-post) so creators can be matched to an ad concept.
- **[`creators.csv`](./creators.csv)** — the structured dataset, 24 scored
  creators sorted by `priority_score` descending.
- **[`METHODOLOGY.md`](./METHODOLOGY.md)** — discovery method, scoring model,
  contact-data guardrails, and data-quality / confidence notes.

## Read this first (honesty caveats)
- Built from **public pages only**; no logins, captchas, or anti-bot measures
  were bypassed. Because YouTube/TikTok/Kick/Twitch/X/Social Blade block
  automated fetching, **most follower counts are `N/A` or `ESTIMATE`** and
  `engagement_rate` is `N/A` (imputed neutral in the composite). Re-verify live
  before outreach.
- Only **publicly-listed business contacts** were captured (one creator, ChrisG,
  has a public email). No private/personal contact info was sought.
- This is a gambling-adjacent category. A **brand-safety / disclosure-compliance
  review is non-optional** before any partnership — see the report's synthesis
  and the FTC/CSGOLotto precedent.
