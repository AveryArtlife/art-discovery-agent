## Cross-cutting observations

1. **Peptide pricing bands (third-party, 2026):** sermorelin $79-$400/mo (telehealth cluster $126-$225); NAD+ injections $119-$395/mo; BPC-157 ~$350/15 mg vial (Marek) to bundled $150-$400/mo (Peter MD). Membership fees ($75-$149/mo) frequently double the effective cost - Ivím, Eden, Hone, Lifeforce.
2. **Regulatory exposure:** Marek, Peter MD, Defy, Gameday and (per conflicting reports) Hone list BPC-157 while it sits between Category 2 removal (Apr 2026) and final FDA action after the July 2026 PCAC vote. Alpha MD and Hims explicitly wait for FDA clearance - a defensible compliance posture.
3. **Vertical integration is the 2026 theme:** Hims (own pharmacies/US manufacturing), Noom (Tailor Made Compounding), Hone (ivee phlebotomy), Function (Getlabs, Ezra, SuppCo), Superpower (proprietary peptides planned).
4. **Diagnostics-first players (Function, Superpower, Mito, Lifeforce) are converging on the same funnel** - annual labs -> protocol -> Rx - with Function the notable holdout on prescribing.
5. **Review divergence** (TRT Nation: Trustpilot 1.9 vs Birdeye 4.9) is a due-diligence flag for curated review widgets across the budget-TRT tier.
