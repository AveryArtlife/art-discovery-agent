# GemBreak — Mystery-Pack Buyer Acquisition Strategy

_Compiled 2026-07-24. How to build an extensive list of potential mystery-pack BUYERS from the
competitor-audience data and convert them into signups with promo offers — compliantly and at scale.
This is the B2C (customer) track, separate from the creator/agency (B2B) track._

---

## The core insight (and the trap to avoid)

The competitor-audience data (commenters, taggers, mentioners, hashtag engagers) is a goldmine of
**ready buyers** — people already spending on mystery boxes. **But do NOT cold-DM/email 1,500 strangers
gambling offers.** That's spammy, converts poorly, violates platform ToS + spam law (CAN-SPAM/TCPA),
and gambling-ad rules make it worse. The high-ROI, compliant move is to use this list to **seed paid
audiences and an opt-in funnel** — turn "a list of handles" into "millions of look-alike buyers you can
legally market to."

## What we already have (the seed)

`buyer_prospects.csv` — **1,492 mystery-box-interested consumers** (687 IG competitor-commenters +
805 X competitor-mentioners), 46 repeat-engaged (hottest). This grows fast with the pending TikTok
comment harvest + competitor follower/liker scrapes.

## The 3 activation channels (ranked by ROI)

### 1) Custom + Lookalike Audiences — the primary engine (compliant, scalable)
- Upload the prospect list (handles → matched via email/phone where we get them, or as a seed) to
  **Meta Ads (Custom Audience)** and **TikTok Ads**.
- Build **1–3% Lookalike Audiences** — Meta/TikTok find *millions* of people who behave like
  competitor buyers.
- Run **promo-offer ads** (free pack / deposit match) to those lookalikes. This is how you reach the
  *whole* market, not just 1,492 people — and it's fully within platform rules.

### 2) Opt-in promo funnel — turns strangers into subscribers you can legally message
- Ads + creators drive to a **landing page**: *"Free pack when you sign up"* / *"100% deposit match."*
- Capture **email/phone with explicit consent + an age-gate** at opt-in.
- Now you can **legally email/SMS** promos (CAN-SPAM/TCPA compliant, because they opted in) and run a
  full nurture sequence. This is the only compliant path to direct messaging.

### 3) Organic warm engagement — small volume, high touch
- The **46 repeat-engagers** and top commenters: engage authentically from the GemBreak brand account
  (reply, react, soft-invite), **not** mass cold-DM. A genuine "you'd love this" comment converts;
  a copy-paste DM gets reported.

## Promo offers that sign buyers up (lowest friction first)
1. **Free first pack / no-deposit bonus** — zero risk to try = highest signup rate.
2. **Deposit match** (100% up to $X) — rewards the first spend.
3. **Refer-a-friend** — viral loop; existing buyers become acquisition.
4. **Creator-code tie-in** — "use [their favorite creator]'s code" = social proof + ties the two
   tracks together (the influencer campaign *is* buyer acquisition).

## The data → buyer pipeline
```
scrape competitor engagers → dedupe → hash + upload as Custom Audience → Lookalike (1-3%)
   → promo-offer ads → landing page (AGE-GATE + opt-in) → email/SMS nurture → first pack → VIP/retention
```

## Compliance — non-negotiable (gambling-adjacent)
- **18+/21+ age-gate** at every entry point.
- **No unsolicited cold email/SMS** to non-opted-in individuals — funnel-first, always.
- **Platform gambling-ad policies** (Meta/TikTok restrict gambling): lead with the **collectibles /
  entertainment / unboxing** framing, geo-target to permitted jurisdictions, and have RG (responsible-
  gambling) messaging ready. Legal review before spend.
- Honor unsubscribe/opt-out instantly; suppress under-age and restricted geos.

## Grow the buyer list bigger (next scrapes, buyer-focused)

- **Competitor post LIKERS** and **followers** — broader audience seed (ToS-sensitive; use for

- Each new batch appends to `buyer_prospects.csv` → refreshes the Custom Audience → improves lookalikes.
