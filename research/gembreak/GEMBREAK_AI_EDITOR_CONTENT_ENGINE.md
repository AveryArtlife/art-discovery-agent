# GemBreak — AI Editor Content Engine (format-jacking, zero filming)

_Compiled 2026-07-28. Rebuilt for an AI video editor, not human creators. The model: take a PROVEN viral
format (brainrot sludge, shock hook, TTS story, satisfying comp) and inject a GemBreak pack-opening clip
as the payload. No one films anything — the editor assembles from sourceable parts + your clip library.
Each template is a repeatable recipe you can spin into 50 variants._

---

## How the engine works
```
INPUT: a library of pack-opening clips (reveals, big pulls, reactions, near-misses, value stamps)
   → pick a TEMPLATE below
   → AI editor sources the parts (gameplay b-roll, TTS, trending audio, text overlays, stock)
   → assembles per the recipe, drops the pack clip in the marked slot
   → spins N variants (swap hook line / bg clip / audio)
   → post at volume; kill losers, put ad spend behind winners
```
**The pack clip is never the whole video — it's the payoff/bottom-half/reveal.** The viral *format* does
the reach; the clip does the conversion.

### The only hard rule: the text/TTS lines must sound HUMAN
The one thing that outs AI content is the words. Enforce this on every hook/TTS line:
- lowercase, blunt, a little broken. "turned my last $40 into this" ✅ — "You won't BELIEVE what I pulled!" ❌
- no marketing voice, no "insane deal," no exclamation stacks, no "link in bio."
- if a line sounds like an ad, cut it. The visual sells; the words just stop the scroll.

## TIER 1 — BRAINROT / SLUDGE (highest retention, run the most)

**1. Split-screen sludge (the staple)**
- MECHANIC: top clip = your reveal; bottom = hypnotic gameplay (Subway Surfers, Minecraft parkour, GTA ramps). Bottom half holds the scroll, top half pays off.
- PARTS: 1 pack-reveal clip (top), 1 loopable gameplay strip (bottom), captions auto-burned, trending sound low under.
- HOOK (0–2s, on-screen): `he opened one pack on his lunch break` → reveal hits ~3–5s in.
- PACK CLIP: top half, full duration.
- LENGTH 8–15s. VARIANTS: swap bottom clip, swap hook line, swap which pull.

**2. Triple-stack sludge**
- MECHANIC: 3 horizontal strips — reveal + gameplay + a satisfying strip (soap cut, kinetic sand). Overstimulation = watch-time.
- HOOK: `the middle one is the pull. keep watching` (misdirection).
- PACK CLIP: top or middle strip. LENGTH 10–15s.

**3. "watch the left side" misdirection**
- MECHANIC: two clips side by side; text says watch one, the pull pops on the OTHER — replays to catch it.
- HOOK: `watch the guy on the left` (pull is on the right).
- PACK CLIP: the un-pointed-at side. LENGTH 8–12s.

## TIER 2 — TTS STORY (AI voice + clip payoff)

**4. "$X into this" glow-up**
- MECHANIC: TTS reads a short come-up over the reveal + calm gameplay bg. The number is the hook.
- PARTS: TTS (TikTok "Jessie"-type voice), pack reveal, soft gameplay bg, captions synced.
- HOOK / TTS: *"took my last forty bucks, said one pack. this is what came out."* → reveal on the payoff word.
- PACK CLIP: the reveal lands on the last line. LENGTH 12–20s.

**5. Fake Reddit story card + TTS**
- MECHANIC: Reddit-card graphic reads on screen, TTS narrates, pack clip is the "and then this happened."
- HOOK / TTS: *"my roommate wouldn't stop talking about this pack site so i tried one to shut him up."* → pull.
- PACK CLIP: payoff at the story's turn. LENGTH 15–25s.

**6. "down bad" list → redemption**
- MECHANIC: TTS lists small Ls over b-roll, then the pack pull as the one W at the end.
- HOOK / TTS: *"lost my parlay. phone at 3%. down to my last forty."* → clean pull.
- PACK CLIP: the final beat. LENGTH 12–18s.

## TIER 3 — SHOCK / RAGEBAIT (comment-farming = reach)

**7. Shock-claim hook → hard cut**
- MECHANIC: a claim that stops the scroll, immediate cut to the pull that proves it. People argue in comments.
- HOOK: `there's a real rolex in a $40 pack and i can prove it` → cut to the watch pull.
- PACK CLIP: the proof cut. LENGTH 8–14s.

**8. "this shouldn't be legal"**
- MECHANIC: over the reveal, framing bait that invites "how is this allowed" comments.
- HOOK: `not sure how this is even allowed` → the pull + value stamp.
- PACK CLIP: under the text. LENGTH 8–12s.

**9. Wrong-on-purpose ragebait**
- MECHANIC: intentionally "wrong" caption/value to farm angry corrections; the reveal quietly corrects it. (Engagement from people who *have* to correct you.)
- HOOK: `a $40 pack can't pull more than $40 right` → then it pulls $2,400.
- PACK CLIP: the correction. LENGTH 8–12s.

**10. Bait-and-switch smash cut**
- MECHANIC: starts as unrelated scroll-stopper (cooking, cleaning, a fight clip), then smash-cuts to the pull ~2s in.
- HOOK: something totally off-topic on screen → hard cut.
- PACK CLIP: post-cut. LENGTH 7–12s.

## TIER 4 — FAKE SCREEN / TEXT STORY

**11. iMessage argument overlay**
- MECHANIC: animated text thread plays over quiet gameplay bg, resolves into the pull.
- HOOK / text: `"just do ONE pack"` / `"no"` / `"i'll venmo you the 40"` → cut to the pull.
- PACK CLIP: the payoff after the last text. LENGTH 10–16s.

**12. Fake "you won" notification**
- MECHANIC: a phone-notification/def-not-real popup animation stacks over the reveal for the dopamine ping.
- HOOK: notification sound + `pack opened` banner → reveal.
- PACK CLIP: under the banner. LENGTH 6–10s.

## TIER 5 — RANKING / COUNTDOWN / LOOP (watch-time hacks)

**13. "top 5 pulls this week" auto-comp**
- MECHANIC: number stickers (5→1), fast-cut compilation of real pulls. Auto-generates weekly from the clip library.
- HOOK: `top 5 pulls on gembreak this week` → #5.
- PACK CLIP: all five slots. LENGTH 15–25s.

**14. "wait for it" countdown bait**
- MECHANIC: a progress/countdown bar builds tension over gameplay; the pull lands at 0.
- HOOK: `wait for the last one` + a ticking bar.
- PACK CLIP: at the 0 mark. LENGTH 8–15s.

**15. Seamless loop**
- MECHANIC: end frame matches the first frame so the video replays invisibly (crushes watch-time).
- HOOK: mid-action open that resolves back to the start pose.
- PACK CLIP: the loop core. LENGTH 5–8s.

## TIER 6 — SATISFYING / ASMR

**16. Satisfying comp splice**
- MECHANIC: the clean reveal cut into a satisfying strip (soap cutting, slime, kinetic sand, pressure wash).
- HOOK: none / `wait for the pull` — visuals carry it.
- PACK CLIP: the satisfying beat. LENGTH 10–18s.

**17. ASMR unbox**
- MECHANIC: isolate the pack-clip audio (foam, click, reveal), macro shots, zero talking, soft beat under.
- HOOK: `🔊` / no text.
- PACK CLIP: full, audio-forward. LENGTH 15–30s.

## TIER 7 — BEAT-SYNC / MEME EFFECT

**18. Beat-synced pull montage**
- MECHANIC: every pull cut to hit on a trending audio's beat. Pure rhythm + dopamine.
- HOOK: the sound itself is the hook (use a rising trend).
- PACK CLIP: multiple pulls on-beat. LENGTH 8–15s.

**19. Zoom-and-emoji reaction meme**
- MECHANIC: the "🤨🔍" zoom-in effect + emoji stickers layered on the reveal moment.
- HOOK: `wait… is that—` with a zoom on the glowing item.
- PACK CLIP: the reveal, effect layered on. LENGTH 6–10s.

**20. CapCut trending-template drop-in**
- MECHANIC: whatever CapCut template is trending this week, pack clip dropped into the media slot. Fastest possible production.
- HOOK: inherited from the template.
- PACK CLIP: the template's clip slot. LENGTH per template.

## Running it at scale (the AI editor's loop)
- **Clip library first.** Tag every pack-opening clip by type: `reveal · big-pull · near-miss · reaction · value-stamp · luxury`. The editor pulls the right type per template slot.
- **Batch 20–50/day.** Same clip × many templates × many hooks. Volume is the strategy — most die, a few pop.
- **Weekly auto-formats.** #13 (top-5) and #18 (beat montage) regenerate automatically from new clips.
- **Kill/scale.** Anything under a view floor dies; anything that pops gets re-cut into 3 more templates and put behind Spark-Ad spend.
- **Rotate sounds constantly.** The single biggest reach lever is riding a *rising* trending audio — refresh the sound pool every few days.
- **Keep the brand light.** Code on screen, name once. The clip converts; the format reaches. If it reads as an ad, it dies.
