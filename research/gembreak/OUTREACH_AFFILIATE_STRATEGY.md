## 0. The unlock: run everything through The Baddest Agency

Outreach no longer goes out as "GemBreak, a brand cold-emailing you." It goes out as **The Baddest
Agency — an influencer marketing agency that pairs creators with paid brand deals.** This changes
everything:

- **Higher reply rates** — a creator agency offering *paid work* is credible; a brand cold-DM is spam.
- **Margin** — the agency takes **10–20% off the top** of every deal it brokers.
- **Transparency & leverage** — creators share real audience data, engagement, and pricing to get
  represented, which feeds better targeting and future deals.
- **Repeatability** — once a creator is "with the agency," GemBreak is just deal #1; every future
  brand is a warm re-contact, not a new cold start.

**Atlas is the agency's outreach engine.** He emails from his own Baddest Agency inbox, opens the
relationship, and hands warm/negotiating leads to the PA.

## 1. The two-engine commercial model (carried forward)

- **Engine 1 — Affiliate flywheel (default, ~80%):** unique promo code + tracked link; creator earns
  a **signup bounty + % of pack sales**, paid monthly, zero upfront. Tiered 10% → 20%.
- **Engine 2 — Paid amplification (top creators):** flat fee per video + whitelisting rights +
  performance bonus; amplify only proven organic winners as Spark/Partnership Ads.
- **Agency layer:** The Baddest Agency brokers both and takes its 10–20% — so a "$1,000 creator post"
  is billed to GemBreak at a markup, or the commission is netted from the affiliate payouts.

## 3. Atlas "beast mode" — the initial-outreach system

### Channel & setup
- Send from a **warmed Baddest Agency domain** (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), not the primary. Cap **40–75
  personalized sends/day**, ramping.
- **One creator = one unique promo code** (generated by Leo) so every reply is attributable.

### The email = personalized opener + standard body
- **Opener (1–2 sentences, unique per creator):** references their niche, platform, a real stat, or
  the competitor they already promote. This is what makes it *not* look like an AI blast.
- **Body (standard, copy-paste):** The Baddest Agency intro + the GemBreak deal + soft CTA. Same for
  everyone — it's the opener that personalizes it.

### Sequence (per creator)
`T+0 intro → T+3 value/proof follow-up → T+7 offer specifics (code + terms) → T+12 last touch → recycle to Leo/DM`

### Reply handling & guardrails
- **Interested → Atlas sends the 1-pager + their custom code**, books a call for the PA if it's a
  paid (Engine 2) deal.
- **Auto-agree** Engine-1 affiliate signups within terms; **escalate to PA/you** any flat fee > $1,000,
  exclusivity, or off-script asks.
- **Always** attach the affiliate code; **always** require FTC disclosure; **never** promise fake
  earnings.

### What Atlas needs from us (built & ready)
1. `atlas_outreach_emails.csv` — a **ready-to-send, per-creator email** (custom opener + full body).
2. The promo-code schema (Leo generates codes on "yes").
3. Escalation rules (above) + the 1-pager PDF (next step if you want it).

## 4. Metrics
North-star: **cost per signup / cost per pack-sale**, and **agency margin captured**. Track reply %,
signup→live %, revenue per creator, and organic→amplify hit-rate. Weekly scorecard (Leo).

## 5. Compliance
FTC disclosure on every post; 18+/21+ age-gate (gambling-adjacent); authentic pulls only; honor the
agency's transparency promise (real data in, honest terms out).
