## ⚠️ Honesty note
Almost no creator publishes a rate card. The per-post / per-month figures in the
list are **tier-based ESTIMATES** derived from the public sources below, not quotes

## Deal structures common in this space
- **CPA** (cost per acquisition): flat $ per referred depositor.
- **Monthly retainer**: fixed, for stable-viewership streamers.
- **Rev-share**: 20–50% of net revenue from referred players (the norm for
  case-opening/affiliate-code creators in the earlier roster).
- **Comps / free-play**: house-funded balance to gamble on stream (raises the
  "are they wagering their own money?" disclosure question).

## Crawl limitation (why this isn't per-creator exact)
Direct crawling of TikTok/Instagram/Kick profiles, Social Blade, Reddit threads and
Discords is blocked by anti-bot walls (and Discord scraping violates ToS), so exact
live counts and private rate cards could not be pulled per creator. The above is the
**publicly documented** pricing picture; an authenticated influencer-analytics tool
(Modash, HypeAuditor, Collabstr) is the unlock for exact per-creator rates.
