- **Keep the hard gambling mechanics on the SITE, not the channel.** The channel is
  **collectibles / unboxing / entertainment** — the same framing that passes ad review.
- **No gambling *ads* pointing straight to deposit pages** from the channel; route through content +
  the age-gated funnel.
- **Age-gate + RG messaging** on gambling-adjacent uploads.
- Publish a couple of "safe" bridge videos before going all-in so the niche-conversion doesn't trip an
  automated review. **A struck channel is the one loss that isn't worth any single win.**

## The lines that stay (your own "no US crime" rule)
