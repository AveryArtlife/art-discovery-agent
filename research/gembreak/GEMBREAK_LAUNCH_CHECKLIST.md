# GemBreak — Launch Checklist (T-minus 1 week)

_Compiled 2026-07-28. The vital to-dos to launch safely and not miss anything. 🔴 = do NOT launch without
it (blocker) · 🟡 = important, have it by launch · 🟢 = fast-follow (week 1–2 OK). Work the 🔴 list first._

---

## 1. Legal & Compliance — the real blockers (a mistake here ends the company)
- 🔴 **Counsel sign-off on the "not gambling" structure** — confirm the three-part cover holds: (a)
  **guaranteed item of value** on every open, (b) **published odds + values before purchase**, (c) a
  **"no purchase necessary" / AMOE** free-entry path. Get it in writing before you take a dollar.
- 🔴 **Age-gate 18+/21+** at signup (DOB capture) — the FTC sent age-verification warning letters across
  this category in 2024; a weak gate is a direct enforcement target.
- 🔴 **Geo-block restricted states** — block **Washington (RCW 9.46 ban)** and **Idaho** at minimum;
  confirm the current list with counsel (monitor CA/NY/CT/MA/MD/NJ sweepstakes activity).
- 🔴 **Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Odds/Buyback disclosure, Refund policy** published and linked —
  including "all sales final," how buyback value is set, and shipping terms.
- 🔴 **Corporate entity + bank account + business insurance** in place (the US-LLC + offshore structure
  per counsel).
- 🟡 **KYC/ID verification** wired to trigger at thresholds (big wins / first withdrawal, ~$500+).
- 🟡 **Responsible-play / self-exclusion** messaging + a spend/limit path (both compliance posture and
  brand-trust).
- 🟡 **AMOE mechanics actually work** — a real, testable free mail-in/online entry path, documented.
- 🟢 **Marketing/FTC compliance:** #ad disclosure baked into the creator/affiliate contract templates.

## 2. Product & Tech — must work on day one
- 🔴 **Provably-fair engine live and verifiable** — server-seed hash shown pre-open, editable client seed,
  nonce, seed rotation + a public verifier page. Test that a user can independently verify a past open.
- 🔴 **The core loop works end-to-end:** browse → see odds → pay → reveal → **ship OR sell-back** → credit
  balance → re-open. Test every branch on staging AND production with real money.
- 🔴 **Odds and displayed retail values are correct on every pack** — a wrong odds/value is both a legal
  and a P&L landmine. Double-check the math per pack.
- 🔴 **Buyback pricing engine** wired to the value source (PGA Value Guide / your feed) and shown to the
  user before they decide. Confirm the spread math.
- 🟡 **Load/stress test** — launch traffic (paid + creator spikes) won't crash checkout or the reveal.
- 🟡 **Mobile-perfect** — most traffic is mobile; test the full flow on real phones (iOS + Android).
- 🟡 **Email/SMS transactional flows** — order confirmation, reveal receipt, shipping, buyback confirmation.
- 🟢 **Case Battles** — Phase 2 after counsel; NOT required for launch.

## 3. Inventory & Fulfillment
- 🔴 **Enough real inventory to cover every pack's odds** — you must be able to deliver any item a pack can
  reveal, including the advertised grails (or a documented, compliant substitution/backorder policy).
- 🔴 **Fulfillment path tested** — who ships, packaging, tracking, insurance on high-value items. Do one
  real test shipment end-to-end.
- 🔴 **Per-pack unit-economics ledger** — acquisition cost, expected resale, odds, projected margin,
  refund rate. This is the operating backbone; portfolio drift kills operators.
- 🟡 **Grail hero item(s) secured** for the launch marketing moment (the Diamond-tier pull that becomes the
  viral clip).
- 🟡 **Supplier accounts open** and reorder path confirmed (authorized wholesale / closeout / dropship).
- 🟡 **Authenticity guarantee** + counterfeit-check process for premium items.
- 🟢 **Open-box / secondary line** for sold-back inventory (can come after launch).

## 4. Payments & Finance
- 🔴 **Payment processing live and tested** — card (expect MCC scrutiny) **and** a crypto path (BTC/ETH/
  USDT) as backup, since card processors are skittish about this category. Do NOT launch on a single fragile
  processor.
- 🔴 **Withdrawal / cash-out flow works** with a daily cap and KYC trigger.
- 🟡 **Chargeback + fraud monitoring** enabled from transaction #1 (this category attracts fraud).
- 🟡 **Wallet/credit balance accounting** reconciles correctly (money in = money tracked).
- 🟢 **Tax/1099 handling** for high-value winners (fast-follow with accountant).

## 5. Trust & UX (this is the brand wedge in a scammy category)
- 🔴 **Transparent buyback number shown** — your differentiator vs IcyBox's opaque valuations. Make it
  visible and honest.
- 🟡 **Odds + values visible before purchase** on every pack page (also a compliance requirement).
- 🟡 **Social proof + reviews path** ready (real reveals, shipping proof) to counter "scam?" skepticism.
- 🟡 **Support channel live** — email/Discord/chat, with response SLAs, before you drive traffic.
- 🟢 **FAQ / how-it-works / provably-fair explainer** pages.

## 6. Fraud, Risk & Support controls
- 🔴 **Fraud/KYC/chargeback controls "ahead of growth"** — velocity limits, device/IP checks, self-referral
  and bonus-abuse detection on the promo codes.
- 🟡 **New-customer-only promo codes** + coupon-leak monitoring + clawback clause in affiliate terms.
- 🟡 **Support runbook** for disputes, failed shipments, buyback complaints.
- 🟢 **Blocklist / VIP flags** for problem accounts and whales.

## 7. Marketing & Launch (the demand side)
- 🔴 **Launch content ready to ship day one** — the clipping engine loaded (brainrot/AI/meme + pack-clip
  splice), plus 2–3 hero reveal clips. Don't launch to silence.
- 🟡 **Creator deals papered** — the first wave signed (codes minted by Leo), assets + FTC terms sent via
  Atlas. Even 3–5 live creators at launch beats zero.
- 🟡 **Affiliate program live** — codes work, tracking attributes, the Deal Menu + agreement ready.
- 🟡 **Paid media accounts warmed** — pixel installed and firing, first campaigns built to the $50 CAC
  target, creative tested (collectibles framing, not gambling).
- 🟡 **Landing / free-first-pack funnel** with the age-gate + opt-in capture live (this manufactures the
  email/PII for lookalikes).
- 🟢 **The 414K YouTube channel** primed (bridge posts, first live break scheduled) + Google audience seed
  building.

## 8. Analytics & Ops
- 🔴 **Analytics + conversion tracking live** — you cannot read CAC in weeks 3–4 (the whole plan's
  benchmark) if tracking isn't firing from minute one. Verify pixel + funnel events before launch.
- 🟡 **Dashboards** for CAC, conversion, repeat-open %, contribution per pack, buyback rate.
- 🟡 **Daily standup + on-call** for launch week (someone watching payments, fraud, support, uptime).
- 🟢 **Weekly scorecard** cadence (Leo) set up.

---

## Launch-week runbook (the 7-day countdown)
- **T-7:** Final counsel sign-off (structure, states, ToS). Lock the 🔴 legal list. Confirm inventory
  covers all odds.
- **T-5:** Full end-to-end money test on production (pay → reveal → ship → buyback → withdraw). Fraud/KYC
  live. Payments backup (crypto) tested.
- **T-3:** Creator wave live (codes + assets), affiliate tracking verified, paid pixel firing, launch
  content queued.
- **T-2:** Load test. Support channel + runbook live. Analytics/dashboards verified.
- **T-1:** Soft-launch to a small real-money cohort (friends/small paid test) — catch anything the tests
  missed. Hero grail secured for the launch clip.
- **T-0 (Launch):** Turn on paid + creators + content engine. War-room monitoring payments, fraud, uptime,
  support. Capture the first big reveal → clip → amplify.
- **T+3 to T+7:** First CAC read; kill/scale creatives; fix the top-3 support/fraud issues; refresh content.

---

### The one-line filter
**If it's on the 🔴 list, it is a launch blocker — legal structure, working provably-fair loop, correct
odds/values, real inventory to cover the odds, working payments + withdrawal, fraud/age controls, live
analytics, and content ready to ship. Everything else can iterate; these cannot be wrong on day one.**
