# GemBreak — 20 Viral Hooks & Scripts (stress-tested, real-creator voice)

_Compiled 2026-07-28. Built from the competitor video teardown (154 scraped TikToks). Written to sound
like an actual creator, not a brand. Drafted ~34, cut everything that pinged the cringe/AI filter below,
kept the 20 that survived._

---

## The anti-cringe filter (what got scripts CUT)
Every script had to pass all of these. If it failed one, it was cut or rewritten:
- ❌ **No narrator voice.** "Today I'm opening…", "Let me show you…", "Here's the thing" → dead on arrival.
- ❌ **No forced hype.** "This is INSANE", "You WON'T believe" → nobody talks like that.
- ❌ **No CTA spam.** "comment below", "smash follow", "link in bio" (the *code* on screen is enough).
- ❌ **No shoehorned slang.** Skibidi/rizz/gyat forced in = instant cringe. Slang only where it's real.
- ❌ **No polished full sentences in captions.** Real captions are lowercase, short, a little broken.
- ❌ **No described reactions.** "I couldn't believe it" → just *react*: "bro. no. NO."
- ❌ **No emoji vomit.** 0–2 max.
- ✅ **Hook is visible in the first 1–2 seconds** — a fragment or a visual question, never a sentence.
- ✅ **One brand mention, woven in.** The pull is the punchline; GemBreak is just where it happened.
- ✅ **Let it breathe.** The best ones are 7–15s and barely "about" the product.

> Format legend — **HOOK** = on-screen text at 0–2s · **shots** = the cuts · **caption** = the post
> caption (lowercase on purpose) · **sound** = audio direction.

## STYLE 1 — Culture / meme (highest reach, 5–12s, barely shows the product)

**1. "she doesn't get it"**
HOOK: *me explaining why i need to open one more pack*
shots: creator standing at a Pete-Davidson-conspiracy-corkboard covered in pulls, pointing at red string, dead serious. cut to girlfriend blinking.
caption: `she doesn't get it`
sound: the conspiracy-board audio (the one everyone uses).

**2. "which one r u"**
HOOK: *everyone in a gembreak break:*
shots: rapid 1-sec character cuts — the hype guy ("LETS GOOO"), the "it's rigged" guy, the silent lurker, the one who actually hits and says nothing.
caption: `which one r u. be honest`
sound: quick punchy trending beat.

**3. "we take those"**
HOOK: *opening packs after going 0-12 in ranked to feel something*
shots: creator slumped, controller down, opens phone, does one pack, small W, tiny nod.
caption: `we take those`
sound: melancholy-lofi-flip that drops on the W.

**4. "off MY idea"**
HOOK: *watching someone hit the grail off the pack i told them to open*
shots: creator watching over friend's shoulder, slow zoom on creator's face going from hype → betrayed.
caption: `off MY idea man`
sound: the slow-zoom sad-violin meme.

## STYLE 2 — Relatable / POV skit (7–15s, the viewer sees themselves)

caption: `i was not ready bro`

HOOK: *opening 1 pack 😇 → opening "just one more" 3 hours later 💀*

caption: `who else does this`

**7. "he did this to me"**

caption: `he did this to me and left`

**8. "she's in now"**
HOOK: *trying to explain gembreak to my mom*

**9. "we all felt that"**
HOOK: *the group chat when someone hits a grail*

## How to run these (per the data)
- **Lead with Styles 1 & 2.** They pull ~6–10× the reach and barely feature the product — that's the
  point. Post the reaction/luxury ones for depth, not top-of-funnel.
- **Batch variants.** Shoot one pull, cut it 3 ways with different hooks; let the algo pick, then put ad
  spend behind the winner (Spark Ad).
- **Clip live breaks fast.** The suspense/reaction ones write themselves off a real livestream — cut and
  post within minutes.
- **Keep the brand light.** Code on screen, name said once, never a sales pitch. The moment it feels like
  an ad, it dies.
