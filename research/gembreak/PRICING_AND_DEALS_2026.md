## TL;DR — the positioning insight (this changes your assumption)

Your instinct ("frame as retail/watch, not gaming, to pay less") is **half right**. The research says:

- **Gambling is the most expensive framing** — creators add a large **risk premium** (regulatory
  exposure, platform-ban risk, reputational backlash, FTC disclosure). Correct to avoid it.
- **BUT "watch / luxury" is NOT the cheap option** — luxury/watch/finance creators charge a
  **+20–50% premium** over general lifestyle. Framing as "luxury watch" would actually cost *more*
  than necessary.
- **The genuinely cheapest legitimate framing is "collectibles / trading-card / unboxing product"**
  — a straightforward e-commerce product promo. Unboxing/haul content is **mid-range priced** and,
  conveniently, **the most authentic fit for a mystery-pack site.**

➡️ **Recommendation: position GemBreak as a fun collectibles / mystery-pack *unboxing product*,
not as gambling and not as luxury.** Cheapest *and* most on-brand.

> ⚠️ Honesty/legal caveat: don't *misrepresent* the mechanic to dodge gambling-disclosure rules.
> If the product is gambling-adjacent, creators must still disclose (FTC 2023–24 loot-box guidance).
> "Collectibles unboxing" is a legitimate framing of what GemBreak is — not a loophole.

## 1. Base rates by tier (general/lifestyle, per single deliverable)

| Tier | Followers | Instagram post | TikTok video | UGC (content-for-ads, no posting) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nano** | <5K–10K | $100–400 | $150–600 | $75–300 / video |
| **Micro** | 10K–100K | $150–1,500 | $200–2,000 | $150–1,000 / video |
| Mid | 100K–500K | $1,500–6,000 | $1,500–5,000 | $1,000–3,000 |

- **UGC ≠ influencer rate.** "UGC creator" pricing is **per-deliverable** (a video the brand runs as
  an ad), typically **$100–500/video** ($350–500 top-tier), and is *based on the content*, not their
  follower count. Influencer rates are *based on reach*. GemBreak will use both models.
- **Usage rights are the #1 hidden cost** — reusing a creator's video in paid ads / on-site / email
  adds a fee (often +25–100% of the base).

## 3. Gambling-specific deal models (context, since GemBreak is gambling-adjacent)

- **Mid-tier casino streamers: $10K–100K / month**; top-tier $1M+/month (e.g. Trainwreck's Stake
  deal scaled to ~$22.5M/mo at peak; Adin Ross ~$1M/week; his Rainbet switch valued ~$100M).
- **Performance models are the norm in gambling:**
  - **CPA (cost per acquisition):** ~**$35–180 per new depositing player** (£35–180), scales with
    creator leverage.
  - **RevShare:** creator takes **20–50%** of net revenue from referred players.
  - Most casino deals are **hybrid** (small base + CPA/revshare + affiliate code).
- **Mystery-box data points:** a 4.7M-sub YouTuber *declined* a **$100K** mystery-box offer; a
  10.8M-sub creator reportedly got **>$100K**. Affiliate/promo codes are standard, and boxes sent to
  creators are commonly stacked with "grails" for good-looking pulls (a practice to avoid — brand-safety/FTC risk).

## 4. Deal structure — advance, bonuses, settlement window

### Advance / upfront
- **Industry standard for new brand↔creator deals: 50% upfront at signing, 50% on completion.**
- Milestone alternative: **25% signing / 25% brief approval / 25% draft delivery / 25% publish.**
- Talent managers usually **insist on ≥50% upfront or escrowed milestones**; nano/UGC creators
  sometimes accept less for a first collab.

### Performance / view-threshold bonuses
- Canonical structure: **"$1,000 base + $300 bonus if the Reel exceeds 50,000 views within 14 days."**
  (bonus ≈ **20–35% of base**, tied to a view target inside a **~14-day window**).
- Sales-based variant: **base + 12% commission on attributed sales in a 30-day window**, plus tiered
  bonuses (e.g. **+$500 if attributed revenue >$10K, +$1,000 if >$25K**).
- Brands increasingly **release ~50% of the fee only after reach/engagement goals are met.**

### Second-payment settlement window (the "when do we pay the rest")
- **Net-30 is the default** — remaining balance within **30 days of the content going live.**
- Brands routinely push **Net-60 / Net-90 / even Net-120**; creators charge **5–15% more** to accept
  net terms with no upfront (cash-flow compensation).
- **View-bonus settlement** typically lands with the second payment, **after the 14-day** measurement
  window closes.

## 5. Suggested GemBreak deal template (starter, nano→micro)

| Element | Recommended starting point |
|---|---|
| **Framing** | "Collectibles / mystery-pack unboxing" (baseline pricing) |
| **Base fee** | Nano: $75–300/video · Micro: $200–800/video (collectibles baseline) |
| **Advance** | 50% on signing (or 30% for first-time nano), rest on publish |
| **Performance bonus** | +20–30% of base if video clears an agreed view target within **14 days** |
| **Affiliate code** | Add a tracked promo code + small CPA/rev-share on referred signups |
| **Second payment** | **Net-30** after publish; bonus settled after the 14-day window |
| **Usage rights** | Negotiate paid-ad reuse separately (+25–100%) only when a video performs |
