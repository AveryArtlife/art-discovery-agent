# GemBreak Creator Affiliate Agreement — Terms Template

_Compiled 2026-07-24. The contract layer: the simple deal the creator was pitched, backed by the
detailed mechanics/guardrails that keep it profitable. Administered by The Baddest Agency
(thebaddest.com) for GemBreak. **This is a business term-sheet template, not legal advice — have
qualified counsel review and localize before use.**_

---

## Layer model (how the specifics stay out of the pitch)
- **Email (Atlas):** relationship only, no numbers.
- **Deal Card:** the simple pitch — *"$25 per customer who deposits $50+ and opens a pack, + 5% of their
  spend for life, stepping up to 10%, free pack for followers, no upfront."*
- **This Agreement:** the binding detail. The creator signs this; it governs if the plain-language
  summary and the detailed terms ever conflict (detailed terms control).

---

## 1. Parties & relationship
- **Program Operator:** GemBreak ("Company"). **Administered by:** The Baddest Agency ("Agency",
  thebaddest.com), which manages creator onboarding, codes, and payouts.
- **Creator:** the influencer/affiliate ("you").
- The Agency represents and administers this program; Agency compensation is arranged separately and
  does not reduce the Creator commissions stated below. Nothing here creates employment, partnership, or
  joint venture.

## 2. Plain-language deal summary (for reference; §4–§8 control)
$25 per Qualified Customer · 5% of Customer Net Spend for the life of the account · rate ladder to 10% ·
free starter pack for the Creator's audience · no upfront cost · paid monthly.

## 3. Definitions (the specifics live here)
- **Referred Customer** — a new customer who registers using the Creator's unique code/link within the
  Attribution Window and was not an existing GemBreak customer.
- **Qualified Customer** — a Referred Customer who (a) deposits **≥ $50** and (b) opens **at least one
  pack**. Bounty and commission accrue only for Qualified Customers.
- **Net Deposits / Net Spend** — the customer's cumulative deposits **minus** withdrawals, cash-outs,
  refunds, chargebacks, and bonus credits, over the measurement period. **All commission percentages are
  calculated on Net Deposits, not on gross pack purchases or wager volume.**
- **Active Customer** — a Qualified Customer with positive Net Spend activity in the trailing 30 days
  (used for the rate ladder).
- **Whale Customer** — a Qualified Customer whose cumulative Net Spend reaches **$1,000+**.
- **Commission Period** — calendar month.
- **Attribution Window** — code-based (permanent to the account) plus a **[30–90]-day** click cookie;
  code takes precedence.

## 4. Commission — bounty
**$25** per Qualified Customer, credited in the Commission Period in which qualification completes.
One bounty per unique customer; no bounty for re-registrations or self-referrals.

## 5. Commission — revenue share (the "5% for life")
- **5%** of each Referred Customer's **Net Deposits**, ongoing for the life of the account, paid monthly.
- **Rate ladder** (applied to the Creator's whole book going forward once a threshold is met):
  - **Starter — 5%** (default)
  - **Pro — 7%** at **25+ Active Customers**
  - **Elite — 10%** at **100+ Active Customers**
- **Whale kicker:** the rate on any individual **Whale Customer** is **10%**, applied automatically from
  the period the customer crosses $1,000 Net Spend, regardless of the Creator's ladder level.

## 6. Qualification, payment & minimums
- Commission and bounty accrue only for **Qualified Customers** (§3).
- **Payout:** monthly, ~**[Net-15]** after period close, once the balance exceeds a **$[50] minimum
  payout threshold** (else it rolls forward).
- **Method:** [PayPal / Wise / bank] as elected at onboarding; Creator is responsible for their taxes and
  for providing required tax forms.

## 7. Negative carryover (loss protection)
If, in any Commission Period, a Creator's Referred Customers collectively have **negative Net Deposits**
(withdrawals/refunds/chargebacks exceed deposits), the negative balance **carries forward** and is offset
against future commissions before further payout. No commission is owed on a net loss.

## 8. Chargebacks, refunds, fraud & clawback
- Commissions on any deposit later **refunded, charged back, or reversed** are deducted (or clawed back
  if already paid).
- **Prohibited:** self-referral; incentivized/bot/fake signups; posting the code to coupon/deal
  aggregator sites; misrepresenting the offer or earnings. Codes are **new-customer-only**.
- Company/Agency may **withhold, adjust, or claw back** commissions and terminate for suspected fraud or
  abuse, and may audit accounts tied to a code.
- **KYC at cash-out:** customer payouts are subject to identity/age verification; commissions on
  customers who fail KYC are void.

## 9. Promo code & attribution
- Creator receives **one unique code + tracked link** (minted by the Agency).
- Attribution is code-first; the code confers the audience perk (free starter pack) and binds the
  customer to the Creator's account permanently, subject to §8.

## 10. Creator obligations (compliance — required)
- **FTC disclosure:** clear, conspicuous **#ad/#sponsored** on every promotional post (and equivalent on
  video/live).
- **No fabricated outcomes:** no faked/staged "wins" presented as real; no misleading earnings or
  guaranteed-value claims.
- **No targeting minors:** do not direct GemBreak promotion at anyone under **[18/21]**; audience must
  meet the age gate.
- **Brand safety:** no illegal, hateful, or deceptive content; follow platform rules.
- Breach of §10 is grounds for immediate termination and forfeiture of unpaid commissions.

## 11. Term, termination & changes
- **Term:** ongoing until terminated by either party on **[14]** days' notice.
- Company/Agency may **modify prospective commission terms** on **[30]** days' notice; changes are not
  retroactive to already-accrued commissions.
- On termination for cause (fraud/§10 breach), unpaid commissions may be forfeited; on ordinary
  termination, accrued and qualified commissions are paid out per §6.
- **Exclusivity:** [Optional — none by default. If a paid/whitelisting deal applies, exclusivity terms
  are set in that separate agreement.]
