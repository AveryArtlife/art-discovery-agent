## 1. Launch philosophy [SR]

Momentum over moment. The category is crowded with one-announcement launches that spike and decay. Reserve Clinic should build a compounding base of owned audience, clinical readiness, and trust infrastructure before any celebrity-driven awareness is switched on. Every rented or borrowed impression is routed into an owned asset (email, SMS, member community, library).

Channel structure:
- **Owned:** ReserveClinic.com, peptide library, email, SMS, member dashboard/community, founder and clinician video.
- **Rented:** Meta, TikTok, YouTube, Google Search (subject to healthcare policy/LegitScript certification), programmatic, podcast ads.
- **Borrowed:** Podcasts (guest appearances), clinicians and practitioner referrals, creators, strategic partners (gyms, longevity clinics, men's-health communities), press.

Non-negotiables: medically reviewed content only; no prescription-volume KPIs; no celebrity content without contract and disclosure; no launch in a state until operational readiness is signed off by the medical director and compliance lead.

## 8. Pricing research plan [SR]
1. Competitive price bands (from `03_Peptide_Product_and_Pricing_Audit.xlsx`) define the floor/ceiling per program.
2. 20–30 customer interviews across three segments (recovery-focused athletes 30–45; metabolic-health seekers 35–55; healthy-aging optimizers 40–60).
3. Van Westendorp price-sensitivity survey (n≥200 per program) for all-in monthly program price and membership price.
4. MaxDiff on program components (clinician access, labs, monitoring cadence, education, member pricing) to build bundles.
5. Landing-page A/B tests of price presentations (all-in vs. itemized) with waitlist conversion as proxy.
6. Cost floor from signed pharmacy, clinician, lab, payment, and support costs (target ≥55% gross margin).
7. Regulatory/support cost loading (consent, monitoring, AE handling) included before setting price.
8. Decision: price set by GM with medical director and compliance sign-off; revisit quarterly.

## 9. Go/No-Go checklist (must be complete before accepting the first customer)

1. Corporate structure executed and counsel opinion on CPOM/fee-splitting for each pilot state.
2. Affiliated professional entity formed; medical director appointed with verified license(s), board status, and malpractice coverage.
3. Formulary approved: every offered ingredient classified (FDA-approved / compounded-permissible / excluded); FDA-restricted bulk substances excluded from injectable compounding offerings.
4. Pharmacy partners licensed in each pilot state (verified via state boards); 503A/503B roles defined; quality agreements and recall procedures signed.
5. Telehealth vendor/EHR/e-Rx live; identity verification live; state-specific consents versioned and logged.
6. Privacy program: HIPAA policies (practice), BAAs signed, consumer-health-data policy live, CMP configured, DSAR process tested.
7. Claims review: all site copy, ads, and creator scripts reviewed against FTC Health Products guidance and Endorsement Guides; disclosures for principals and creators live.
8. Auto-renewal compliance: pre-checkout disclosure, affirmative consent, easy cancellation, renewal reminders per state law.
9. Payments: processor approval in writing for the business model; backup processor; chargeback plan.
10. Insurance bound: professional liability (practice and clinicians), product liability, cyber, D&O, general.
11. Adverse-event and pharmacovigilance SOP tested; 24/7 clinical escalation path staffed.
12. Accessibility audit passed (WCAG 2.2 AA), security pen test passed, incident-response plan approved.
13. Support staffed to SLA; refund/return policies published.
14. Trademark application filed and clearance memo received for RESERVE CLINIC (RESERVE is a common mark element; CLINIC is descriptive); counsel opinion on use of 'clinic' in the trading name by entity and state; domain and social handles secured.
15. Principals: identity verified; credentials verified; agreements signed with NIL, morality, exclusivity, FTC disclosure, termination and post-termination use terms; or Scenario B activated and all principal references removed.
16. LegitScript certification granted (required before paid search on Google/Meta for prescription-related services) — or paid search deferred.
17. Medical Director written sign-off; Compliance Lead written sign-off; GM written sign-off.

## 10. Crisis and reputation readiness [SR]
Holding statements for: adverse event, pharmacy recall, regulatory inquiry, principal controversy, data incident, negative investigative press. Spokesperson: medical director for clinical topics; GM for corporate; principals never speak to clinical matters. Monitoring: brand and principal mentions daily; escalation matrix with 2-hour acknowledgment target.
