# Supplier Outreach Emails

Rules applied to every draft below:

- The end customer is **never** identified. They are *"a large institutional
  customer in Mexico"* or *"a Mexican institutional/government-related customer."*
- **No target price is disclosed.** The supplier reveals their number first.
- The 3,500-unit order is framed as a **qualification order for a substantially
  larger procurement** — true, and it is the entire source of your leverage.
- Tone is a buyer who has done the work, not a tyre-kicker.

**Before sending:** replace `[NAME]`, `[YOUR NAME]`, `[YOUR TITLE]`,
`[YOUR COMPANY]`, `[PHONE]`, `[EMAIL]`. Attach `02-rfq-technical-specification.md`

## EMAIL #1 — U.S. manufacturers

**To:** AmeriQual, SOPAKCO, Baxters North America (Wornick), XMRE/NEX-XOS, MRE STAR
**Subject:** RFQ – Individual MRE Meals – Initial 3,500 Units / Large Follow-On Requirement – Mexico

---

Dear [NAME],

We are sourcing an individual MRE-style ration on behalf of a large institutional
customer in Mexico and would like to request a quotation.

**Requirement**

The unit of supply is **one individually sealed meal packet** — not a 24-hour
ration pack. End users receive three individual meals per day.

- **Immediate requirement:** 3,500 individual meals
- **Expected follow-on:** potentially 100,000 to 1,000,000+ individual meals,
  subject to successful product qualification

To be direct about the 3,500 units: it is a **product qualification order** ahead
of a substantially larger procurement. We are running it against a small number of
qualified manufacturers, and performance on this order — product, documentation and
lead time — determines who we position for the volume tiers.

**Configurations**

We would like pricing on two builds:

- **Configuration A** — value: approximately 900–1,100 kcal per meal
- **Configuration B** — premium: approximately 1,200–1,350 kcal per meal,
  functionally comparable to a current U.S. military MRE

Configuration B is our primary interest. Full specifications are attached.

**One point we want to raise directly.** We are seeking **fresh commercial or
export production only**. We are not seeking U.S. Government surplus, government-
owned stock, or any product bearing U.S. Government property markings, and we
cannot accept such product for this customer. Please confirm that anything you
offer is commercially produced and newly manufactured.

**Please quote per single individual meal, in USD, at each of:**
3,500 / 25,000 / 100,000 / 250,000 / 500,000 / 1,000,000

For each tier: **EXW**, **FOB**, and **CIF Mexico** if offered.

**Also please provide:**

- MOQ and production lead time at each tier
- Monthly production capacity in individual meals
- Shelf life and your **manufacturing date policy** (how fresh is delivered product?)
- Menu list and nutritional data sheets
- Case and pallet configuration; meals per 40 ft container
- FRH included and excluded pricing, and the FRH's transport classification
- Private-label cost and minimum, and **Spanish-language packaging** capability
- Sample availability for 2–5 menus, with cost and lead time
- Food-safety certifications (certificate numbers and expiry dates)
- Military / government references
- Payment terms and price validity

We would appreciate a response within ten business days. Happy to sign an NDA if
that helps you quote in more detail.

Best regards,

## EMAIL #3 — Follow-up / price negotiation

Send **after** the first quote. Do not send this until you have at least two
comparable quotes in hand — the leverage is real only if the alternatives are.

**Subject:** RE: RFQ-MX-MRE-2026-01 – Commercial Review Ahead of Supplier Selection

---

Dear [NAME],

Thank you for your quotation of [DATE]. It has been reviewed alongside offers from
several other qualified manufacturers in [North America / Europe / Asia], and I
want to give you a clear picture of where you stand rather than leave you guessing.

**Where you are strong:** [SPECIFIC — e.g. shelf life, menu range, certifications,
lead time]. That is genuinely differentiating and it is why we are coming back to
you rather than closing the file.

**Where the offer is not yet competitive:** your pricing at the **100,000 and
above** tiers. We are currently evaluating comparable Configuration B offers from
manufacturers with equivalent food-safety credentials and military supply history,
and your 100,000+ pricing is not currently in the range that would support
positioning you for the programme volume.

I want to be precise about how this decision is being made, because I think it
works in your favour:

1. The 3,500-unit qualification order is **not** where this is decided. We are not
   optimising the pilot.
2. Supplier selection is driven by pricing and demonstrated capacity at the
   **100,000 / 250,000 / 500,000 / 1,000,000** tiers.
3. We are selecting a **primary** and a **secondary** manufacturer. Both get
   volume. Only the primary gets first call on the programme.

**What would move you forward:**

- Your genuinely best pricing at 250,000 / 500,000 / 1,000,000, quoted per
  individual meal, EXW and FOB
- Confirmed monthly capacity in individual meals at those tiers, and the earliest
  production slot you can commit to
- Any cost reduction available through specification adjustment — component
  substitution, FRH sourcing, packaging format, menu simplification. **We would
  rather you propose an intelligent cost-down than shave margin.** Tell us what is
  expensive and why
- Volume-commitment or annual-contract pricing, if that structure gives you a
  better number
- Payment-term flexibility, if that supports the price

We are not asking you to match an arbitrary target and we are not going to play
suppliers off against each other on a spreadsheet. We are asking for your real
number at real volume, because that is the number the decision turns on.

Our selection timeline is [DATE]. If you can revise by [DATE − 5 days] we can keep
you in the primary evaluation.
