# Sample Qualification Plan & Scorecard

## 1. Plan

**Objective:** decide, on physical evidence, which manufacturer gets the 3,500-unit
pilot and which gets positioned for 100K+.

**Scope:** top 5 manufacturers × **2–5 menu samples each** (10–25 samples total).

**Benchmark:** the customer's own reference item — a U.S. military MRE, Menu 21
(Tuna, Chunk Light, Lemon Pepper), AmeriQual. **If you can obtain a single
legally-sourced comparable** — a fresh commercial Sure-Pak 12 meal is the closest
legal equivalent — **put it on the table as the control.** Scoring against a
physical control is far more decisive than scoring against a spec sheet.

**Timeline:** request samples week 3, evaluate week 4, decide week 5.

**Panel:** minimum three evaluators, scoring independently before discussing.
Taste scoring is worthless from a single palate. If the customer will send a
representative to the tasting, that is worth more than any other diligence step
in this programme.

**Cost:** budget $150–$400 per supplier including freight. Pay for samples — it
speeds everything up and it signals you are a real buyer.

### Rules that keep the evaluation honest

1. **Blind the taste portion.** Decant entrées into unmarked containers. Brand
   knowledge contaminates taste scores badly.
2. **Weigh and photograph everything before opening.** Component shortfalls are
   the most common quiet defect and they vanish once the pouch is open.
3. **Read and record every date code.** A supplier that sends 3-year-old samples
   is telling you exactly what they will ship on the order.
4. **Score the packaging before the food.** For a field ration, packaging failure
   is a worse outcome than mediocre taste.
5. **Test one FRH per supplier in realistic conditions** — cold water, outdoors,
   timed.
6. **Do not score suppliers against each other on price during the tasting.**
   Score product first, then overlay price. Otherwise the cheap sample wins on
   halo and you lose the objectivity you paid for.

## 2. Scorecard

Score each criterion **1–10**. Weights sum to 100.

| # | Criterion | Weight | What a 10 looks like | What a 1–3 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Packaging integrity** | 8 | Rugged, fully sealed, no delamination, survives drop and crush | Thin film, weak seals, punctures |
| 2 | **Total weight vs declared** | 4 | Within ±3% of declared | >10% under declared |
| 3 | **Calories vs declared** | 8 | Verified 1,200–1,350 kcal, matches data sheet | Materially below declared |
| 4 | **Taste — entrée** (blind) | 10 | Genuinely good hot or cold | Unpalatable |
| 5 | **Entrée size / portion** | 8 | ≥300 g, visibly substantial | <200 g, sauce-heavy |
| 6 | **Side components complete** | 7 | Every specified component present | Missing components |
| 7 | **FRH performance** | 7 | Reliable ignition, no leakage, safe | Fails, leaks, or is dangerous |
| 8 | **Heating time** | 4 | ≤12 min to serving temperature | >20 min or inadequate heat |
| 9 | **Shelf life stated** | 7 | 36 months, with validation study | <24 months or unsupported |
| 10 | **Manufacturing date of sample** | 7 | <90 days old | >12 months old |
| 11 | **Label quality & accuracy** | 5 | Clear, accurate, complete nutritionals | Illegible, inaccurate, incomplete |
| 12 | **Spanish customisation demonstrated** | 6 | Spanish sample supplied or firm commitment + cost | Cannot or will not |
| 13 | **Ease of use in the field** | 5 | Opens by hand, logical sequence, usable one-handed | Needs tools, awkward |
| 14 | **Military appearance / discretion** | 3 | Matte, low-signature, professional | Consumer-retail styling |
| 15 | **Durability (drop / crush / 24 h water)** | 6 | Survives all three | Fails any |
| 16 | **Case packing quality** | 3 | Strong case, clear markings, dated | Weak, unmarked, undated |
| 17 | **Certifications supplied with sample** | 2 | Full set, with numbers and expiry | None supplied |
| | **TOTAL** | **100** | | |

### Physical tests to actually perform

| Test | Method | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| Drop | 1.5 m onto concrete, flat and corner | No seal failure |
| Crush | 50 kg static load, 60 seconds | No rupture |
| Water immersion | Submerge 24 h | Contents dry |
| Thermal | 24 h at 50 °C, then inspect | No separation, swelling or leakage |
| FRH | Cold water, timed, outdoors | Reaches serving temperature ≤12 min |
| Weight | Scale, compare to declared | Within ±3% |
| Date code | Read and record every code | <90 days, decodable |

## 4. Automatic disqualifiers

Regardless of score:

- [ ] Sample bears any government property marking
- [ ] Sample manufacturing date cannot be read or is >18 months old
- [ ] Declared calories overstated by >10%
- [ ] Certificates cannot be produced with numbers and expiry dates
- [ ] Factory address cannot be confirmed
- [ ] Packaging fails the water immersion test
- [ ] FRH fails or is unsafe
- [ ] Supplier cannot commit to a manufacturing date policy in writing
- [ ] Supplier quotes a 24-hour ration price against an individual-meal line
