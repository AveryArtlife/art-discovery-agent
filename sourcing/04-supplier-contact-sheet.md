## 3. Contact verification protocol — run this before sending

**Per company, in this order. Budget ~10 minutes each.**

**Step 1 — the website (2 min).** Open the site listed above. Check
`/contact`, `/en/contact`, `/about`, and any `defence`, `military`, `government`,
`export` or `international` page. Ration manufacturers frequently keep a separate
defence-sales contact that is not on the main contact page.

**Step 2 — LinkedIn (4 min).** Search the company, open *People*, and filter by
these titles in priority order:

1. Export Sales Manager / Director of Export
2. International Sales Manager
3. Government Sales / Defence Sales / Military Sales
4. Business Development Manager — Defence
5. Commercial Director / Sales Director
6. Key Account Manager — Institutional

For Turkey also try Turkish: *İhracat Müdürü* (export manager), *Savunma Satış*
(defence sales). For Spain: *Director de Exportación*, *Responsable de Ventas
Internacionales*. For Poland: *Dyrektor Eksportu*.

**Step 3 — derive the email pattern (2 min).** Find one confirmed address on the
site, note the pattern (`first.last@`, `finitial+last@`, `first@`), and apply it to
the name from Step 2. **Verify it** with an email-verification tool before sending
— never send blind to a constructed address, because a bounce to a defence-sales
inbox is a bad first impression.

**Step 4 — trade-show directories (2 min).** These publish exhibitor contacts and
are the best single source for defence-food sales staff:

- **IDEX** (Abu Dhabi) — Jomipsa exhibited at IDEX 2023 `FACT (search)`
- **BSDA** (Bucharest) — Jomipsa exhibited `FACT (search)`
- **DSEI** (London), **Eurosatory** (Paris), **SAHA EXPO / IDEF** (Turkey),
  **MSPO** (Poland), **DSA** (Malaysia), **FIDAE / Expodefensa** (Latin America)
- **Anuga / SIAL / Gulfood** for the food-industry side

**Step 5 — official registries for the ones that matter.**

- **ARPOL:** NCAGE **1126H** → look up in the NATO/NSPA codification system
- **Golden Season:** UEN **198403734E** → Singapore ACRA registry
- **UNIFO:** Turkish trade registry / D&B
- **US primes:** SAM.gov entity records carry a points-of-contact section

**Step 6 — the phone call.** For the four priority-tier companies, **call**. Ask
for "the person who handles export sales for defence or institutional customers."
Two minutes on the phone beats a week of email routing, and it is how you find out
whether they will even quote 3,500 units.

## 4. What to say on that first call

> "I'm sourcing an individual MRE-style meal for an institutional customer in
> Mexico. Initial qualification order of 3,500 individual meals, with follow-on
> potential into the hundreds of thousands. I need to reach whoever handles export
> or government sales — who should I be speaking to, and what's the best address
> to send a formal RFQ?"

Then ask two qualifying questions before you invest any more time:

1. **"Do you sell commercially produced product for export, or only under national
   defence contracts?"** — this immediately separates the DLA primes that can serve
   you from the ones that cannot.
2. **"What's your minimum order quantity for an individual meal?"** — if the answer
   is 100,000, they are a follow-on supplier, not a pilot supplier, and you should
   route them accordingly rather than wasting a qualification slot.
