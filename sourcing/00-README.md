## READ THIS FIRST — verification ceiling of this report

This research session had **no direct website access**. The execution environment's
egress policy returned HTTP 403 on every outbound connection attempt
(`ameriqualgroup.com`, `sopakco.com`, `dla.mil`, `mreinfo.com`, and all others tested).
I did not route around it.

All findings therefore come from **search-engine retrieval** — search result titles,
URLs, and the search backend's summaries of page content. That means:

- I could **not** open a single company website, DLA contract PDF, TED notice, or
  LinkedIn profile directly.
- Every company detail, price, address and phone number below is **second-hand**.
- I have **invented nothing**. Where I could not establish a fact, it is marked
  `UNKNOWN`. There are no fabricated email addresses, contract prices, capacities,
  certifications or customer references anywhere in this pack.

**Confidence tags used throughout:**

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `FACT (search)` | Asserted by a named, cited source, retrieved via search. Not independently confirmed against the primary document. |
| `ESTIMATE` | My calculation or judgement, with the method shown. Never a quotation. |
| `ASSUMPTION` | A working premise that has not been tested. |
| `UNKNOWN` | Could not be established. Do not fill this in by guessing. |

Nothing in this pack is a supplier quotation. Section 12 of the audit lists the
items you must confirm from primary sources before money moves.
