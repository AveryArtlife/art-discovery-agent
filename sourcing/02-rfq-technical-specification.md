# RFQ Technical Specification — Individual MRE-Style Meal
Reference: **RFQ-MX-MRE-2026-01** · Issued: September 2026 · Revision: A

Attach this document to every RFQ. It is written to be sent to suppliers as-is.
It deliberately contains **no target price** and **no end-customer identification**.

---

## 1. Overview

We are sourcing an **individually sealed, shelf-stable, ready-to-eat meal packet**
for a large institutional customer in Mexico.

**This is not a 24-hour ration pack requirement.** The unit of supply is **ONE
individually sealed meal**. End users receive three such meals per day. All
pricing must be quoted **per single individual meal packet**.

- **Initial order:** 3,500 individual meals (product qualification order)
- **Follow-on potential:** 25,000 / 100,000 / 250,000 / 500,000 / 1,000,000+
  individual meals, subject to successful qualification
- **Destination:** Mexico

---

## 2. Reference product

The target is functionally equivalent to a current **U.S. military Meal,
Ready-to-Eat (MRE)** — entrée, side, bread/crackers, spread, dessert or snack,
beverage components, accessory pack, flameless ration heater, in a rugged flexible
outer pouch.

**Literal U.S. MIL-SPEC compliance is not required.** Functional and nutritional
equivalence is acceptable. Suppliers already holding MIL-SPEC or national
defence-standard approvals should say so.

**We require commercially produced product only.** We will not accept, and cannot
purchase, any product bearing U.S. Government property markings or any government-
owned ration stock.

## 5. Flameless ration heater

- Quote **with FRH** and **without FRH** as separate line items on both configurations.
- State the FRH manufacturer and country of origin.
- State heating performance: time to temperature, temperature rise, water volume required.
- **State the FRH's dangerous-goods classification** and any IATA/IMDG restriction
  affecting air or sea transport, including for sample shipments.

## 7. Quality, food safety and provenance

State and provide certificate copies (with certificate numbers and expiry dates):

- HACCP
- ISO 22000 / FSSC 22000 / BRCGS / IFS or equivalent
- Halal and/or Kosher, if held
- National defence-standard approvals (e.g. NATO AQAP, STANAG, national MoD)
- NCAGE code, if held
- Export/veterinary establishment approval numbers for meat-containing products

Also state:

- **Legal company name and the physical address of the factory** performing the retort processing
- Whether you are the manufacturer, the assembler, or both
- Whether you manufacture the entrées yourself or source them, and from where
- Full traceability procedure, lot coding, and mock-recall capability
- Storage temperature requirements and any temperature excursion tolerance
- Shelf-life validation methodology supporting the claimed shelf life

## 9. Mandatory declarations

Confirm explicitly, in writing:

1. All product offered is **commercially produced** and bears **no** government property marking.
2. All product will be **newly manufactured** for this order — not existing inventory — unless we agree otherwise in writing.
3. Minimum **90% of stated shelf life remaining** on arrival at destination.
4. You are able to supply documentation required for import into Mexico, including a **Certificate of Analysis per lot** (physical, chemical and microbiological, from an accredited laboratory), health/veterinary certificates, and certificate of origin.

---

## 10. Evaluation

Offers will be evaluated on price, product match, food-safety credentials,
demonstrated capacity, lead time, customisation capability, and total landed cost
in Mexico. **Failure to provide a factory address, certificate copies, or a
manufacturing date policy will result in exclusion.**
