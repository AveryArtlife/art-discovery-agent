## The master RFQ email

**Subject:** RFQ – Individual MRE Meals – Initial 3,500 Units / Large Follow-On Requirement – Mexico

---

Dear [NAME],

We are sourcing an individual MRE-style ration on behalf of a large institutional
customer in Mexico, and would like to request a quotation.

[TAILORED PARAGRAPH — see per-supplier variants below]

**1. REQUIREMENT**

The unit of supply is **one individually sealed meal packet**. This is not a
24-hour ration pack requirement. End users receive three individual meals per day.

- Initial order: **3,500 individual meals** — a product qualification order
- Expected follow-on: **100,000 to 1,000,000+ individual meals**, subject to successful qualification
- Destination: Mexico

All pricing must be quoted **per single individual meal packet, in USD**. We have
found the individual-meal versus 24-hour-ration distinction to be the most common
source of non-comparable quotations in this category, so please state your unit of
quotation explicitly.

**2. PRODUCT SPECIFICATION**

We request pricing on two configurations.

*CONFIGURATION B — PREMIUM (our primary interest)*

- Energy: **1,200–1,350 kcal** per individual meal
- Main entrée: minimum one retort pouch, minimum 300 g. Please state whether a second retort pouch (starch/staple) can be added, and at what additional cost
- Side dish
- Bread component — crackers, biscuit or tortilla (**tortilla option requested**)
- Spread
- Snack / energy component
- Dessert
- Beverage: powdered beverage, electrolyte, coffee / creamer / sugar
- Accessory pack: spoon, napkin, wet wipe, salt, pepper, sugar
- Condiments: hot sauce or salsa requested
- **Flameless ration heater included in the base quote**, plus a separate price excluding FRH
- Outer pouch: rugged, waterproof / moisture-resistant flexible pouch
- Menus: minimum 12 varieties preferred
- Shelf life: minimum 24 months; **36 months strongly preferred**

*CONFIGURATION A — VALUE*

- Energy: **900–1,100 kcal** per individual meal
- Otherwise as above; single entrée acceptable; FRH quoted separately

Literal U.S. MIL-SPEC compliance is **not** required — functional and nutritional
equivalence is acceptable. If you already hold MIL-SPEC or national defence-standard
approvals, please state them.

**3. PRICING SCHEDULE REQUESTED**

Please quote per single individual meal, in USD, at each of:

**3,500 / 25,000 / 100,000 / 250,000 / 500,000 / 1,000,000**

For each tier: **EXW**, **FOB (named port)**, and **CIF Mexico** if offered.

**4. COMMERCIAL AND TECHNICAL INFORMATION REQUESTED**

1. Minimum order quantity
2. Production lead time at each tier
3. Monthly production capacity, stated in individual meals
4. Earliest available production slot
5. Shelf life, and your **manufacturing date policy** — how fresh will delivered product be?
6. Complete menu list
7. Nutritional data sheet per menu
8. Case configuration: meals per case, case dimensions, case gross weight
9. Pallet configuration: cases per pallet, pallet dimensions and gross weight
10. Meals per 20 ft and 40 ft container
11. FRH: manufacturer, country of origin, heating performance, and **dangerous-goods / transport classification**
12. Private-label cost and minimum quantity
13. **Spanish-language packaging capability**, and whether you have previously produced to Mexican **NOM-051** labelling
14. Sample availability for 2–5 menus, with cost and lead time
15. Food-safety certifications **with certificate numbers and expiry dates** (HACCP, ISO 22000 / FSSC 22000 / BRCGS / IFS)
16. Military, government or institutional references
17. Payment terms
18. Price validity period, and any raw-material escalation mechanism

**5. MANDATORY CONFIRMATIONS**

Please confirm explicitly in your response:

a. All product offered is **commercially produced** and bears **no government property marking** of any kind.
b. All product will be **newly manufactured** for this order rather than supplied from existing inventory, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
c. Minimum **90% of stated shelf life** will remain on arrival at destination.
d. You can supply the documentation required for import into Mexico, including a **Certificate of Analysis per lot** (physical, chemical and microbiological, from an accredited laboratory), health / veterinary certificates, and certificate of origin.

**6. NEXT STEPS**

We would appreciate a response within ten business days. We are happy to sign an NDA
if that allows you to quote in more detail.

If a colleague handles export, defence or government sales, please forward this and
let me know who I should address directly.

Kind regards,

Avery

avery@artlife.com · [Phone]
