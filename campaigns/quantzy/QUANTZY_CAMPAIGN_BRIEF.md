## 0. TL;DR (read this even if you read nothing else)

Our category (crypto / sports betting / retail trading) is loud, sweaty, and self-important. Every competitor is shouting the same three words: **moon, edge, alpha.** They cast either (a) a hoarse bro screaming at a screen or (b) an A-list actor pretending they understand a blockchain. Both are exhausted.

Quantzy wins by walking in at half volume, unimpressed, and quietly correct. Ksenia is the vehicle. Her deadpan is the format. The joke is always the same: **everyone else is performing intelligence; Quantzy just has it.**

Every spot ends the same emotional beat — the viewer feels a little dumber for how hard they were trying, and a little safer for the fact that Quantzy exists.

## 2. What every viral ad in this space actually is

Across ~40 spots, five structural facts show up in almost all of them.

### 2.1 They sell a feeling, never a product
Nobody in a Crypto.com ad ever explains what Crypto.com does. Damon says "brave" fourteen times. The product is a *stance*. Your job as writer: pick the stance first, feature second.

### 2.2 They collapse a huge cultural anxiety into a small clean line
- "Fortune favors the brave." (fear of missing out → self-worth)
- "Don't be like Larry." (fear of being the last one to notice → tribal shame)
- "More ways to win." (fear of losing → reframed as optionality)
- "eToro is you." (fear of being small → ego reinforcement)
- "Our blades are f***ing great." (fear of being sold to → complicity)

Notice: every line reframes a *fear* into a *flattering identity*. That is the trick.

### 2.3 They use the "confused normie" as a hostage
Larry David is the archetype. So is the E*TRADE baby (inversion — the baby is the smart one). So is every Squarespace celebrity pretending not to understand what a website is. **The viewer is invited to feel superior to a character on screen.** That superiority is the emotion they walk away with, and they attach it to your brand.

### 2.4 Numbers are used as texture, not evidence
"340% APY," "3-leg parlay," "$1000 risk-free," "0% commission." None of these are ever verified in the ad. They function like drum hits — rhythmic, specific, credible-adjacent. Use one specific number per spot. Never two.

### 2.5 They perform anti-establishment while being the establishment
Robinhood after GME. FTX literally saying "don't be like the guy who says no to new things." DraftKings implying that MGM is your dad's casino. Every one of these companies is a large, VC- or PE-backed institution pretending to be your slightly-illegal friend. The audience knows and forgives, as long as the tone is consistent.

## 3. Shared elements — the checklist

If a script is missing more than **two** of these, it's not a category ad. Bold = mandatory for Quantzy.

- **Cold open under 3 seconds.** No logo, no vibe-setting, no "In a world…"
- **A single specific number.** ("Up 340%," "1,900 pros," "3 seconds late.")
- **A confused or losing character on screen** the viewer feels smarter than.
- **A hero character who is unbothered.** (Damon, Ray Winstone, E*TRADE baby, Ksenia.)
- **A tribal in-word.** ("Brave." "Print." "Cook." "Edge." "Line." "Alpha.")
- **A meta-wink to the fact that this is an ad.** Even Bet365 does it with Winstone's floating head; the surrealism *is* the wink.
- **A tempo change 2/3 of the way through.** Either a hard cut to silence, or a jump to product beauty shot with music drop.
- **A closer that isn't a CTA.** ("Fortune favors the brave." Not "Download now.")
- **A visual signature.** Damon walking through a museum. Winstone's disembodied head. The E*TRADE crib. Our signature is **Ksenia's face not moving.**
- **Product name spoken minimum 2×, maximum 4×.** More than 4 reads as insecure.

## 4. Psychological subtext — what's actually being pushed

Every ad in this category is running one or more of these levers. Name the lever *before* you write the joke.

| Lever | What it exploits | Category example | How Quantzy uses it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FOMO** | Loss aversion + social proof | Larry David skipping every big invention | Ksenia already got the trade; the bro on screen didn't |
| **Anti-establishment** | Distrust of gatekeepers | Robinhood "we are all investors" | Quantzy = your unfair advantage, minus the Bloomberg terminal |
| **In-group signaling** | Belonging | DraftKings "let's fkn go" | Ksenia refuses to say "let's fkn go" — that IS the in-group signal now |
| **Ego reinforcement** | Self-image as smart money | eToro "eToro is you" | "You are not a genius. Quantzy is." Inverted flattery. |
| **Locus of control** | Illusion of skill | E*TRADE "take control" | Quantzy takes control back from your worst impulses |
| **Fatalism / dark comedy** | Relief through admission | Paddy Power everything | Ksenia's Slavic shrug — "You will lose money. Less, with us." |
| **Complicity / meta** | "We know it's an ad" | Dollar Shave Club, Reynolds | Ksenia acknowledging the camera, the script, the brief |

The two levers Ksenia is uniquely built for and *no competitor is currently pulling*: **fatalism** and **complicity**. Own both.

**Words we own, going forward:**
**intelligence, edge, agent, quiet, correct.** Every spot must land at least three of these five.

## 6. Comedy rules — dry, sarcastic, ironic (Ksenia mode)

This is not gag comedy. Nothing is punched up. The laugh is in the *gap* between what a normal ad would do and what she does instead.

1. **Never break for the joke.** Ksenia does not know she is funny. The camera knows. The edit knows. She doesn't.
2. **Understate the stakes.** "Lost your rent" > "LOST EVERYTHING." A whisper is louder than a scream in this category.
3. **Cultural specificity as texture.** Slavic details land because they are unexpected in a fintech ad. Reference them once per script, never twice. (Borscht, dacha, "my grandmother," a specific city that isn't Moscow.) Never do a fake accent joke — she already has one, that's the whole point.
4. **The setup is a cliché. The payoff is a shrug.** Setup = "Meet Brad. Brad trades on vibes." Payoff = not a punchline, a fact. "Brad is broke."
5. **Silence is a weapon.** Give her a 2-second pause on camera and the viewer fills it with the joke themselves. That's the best joke you'll write.
6. **Never explain the product in the joke.** Explain it in the beauty shot. The joke is real estate for tone, not for features.
7. **The 4th wall is a door, not a wall.** She can address the camera. She cannot wink. Winking is for Ryan Reynolds; Ksenia does not wink.
8. **No wordplay.** No puns. No "Quantzy = quantum + easy." If a line rhymes, cut it.
9. **Punch up, not down.** The target of every joke is (a) the trader-bro archetype, (b) the industry, (c) Ksenia herself. Never the viewer. The viewer is her co-conspirator.
10. **Fatalism > optimism.** She does not promise wealth. She promises less humiliation.

## 7. Format & production rules

- **Aspect:** 9:16 native primary, 1:1 secondary, 16:9 last. Every spot must survive a phone in portrait with sound off for the first 2 seconds (subtitle burn-in mandatory).
- **Length:** :15, :30, :60. Every :60 must have a clean :30 pull and a :15 pull built in — plan the edit points at write time.
- **Sound design:** No stock "trader" sound bed (no ticking, no CNBC synth). Ksenia's voice, room tone, one music cue that arrives at the tempo change. Silence is a design element.
- **Color:** Muted. The category is neon; we are matte. Product UI is the only saturated color on screen.
- **Product on screen:** Real UI, always. No fake charts. If we show a P&L number, it is a real backtested screen.
- **Legal:** "Trading involves risk" super in every spot. Put it under a Ksenia line so it reads as her handing you the disclaimer, not us hiding it.
- **Name enunciation:** "KWAHNT-zee." Rehearse. If she says it wrong once, we're stuck with the wrong pronunciation forever (see: gif).

## 8. What Quantzy is (for the room)

**Product:** AI-powered trading agent. Reads the market at machine speed, executes with rules, keeps a human on the wheel.
**Promise:** Intelligence and edge for people who were never going to build a quant desk.
**Enemy:** The trader-bro industrial complex — the Discord gurus, the "0DTE" TikToks, the sports-betting brainrot bleeding into retail investing.
**Not enemy:** The viewer. Even if the viewer is the trader-bro. Especially then.

The pitch, in one line Ksenia could actually say:
> "Quantzy trades for people who used to trade for themselves. It went about as well as you'd expect."

## 9. Ksenia's voice — a short character bible

- Age-agnostic 30s. Reads late-20s to mid-40s depending on styling.
- Grew up somewhere cold. Never says where.
- Has watched an American man explain crypto to her. Multiple times.
- Reads more than she talks. Talks less than the script says.
- Wardrobe: matte black, one interesting piece (a coat, a scarf, a single earring). No logos, no watch brands.
- Body: still. Hands stay in pockets or on a cup. She does not gesture to sell a line.
- Face: one microexpression per beat. Eyebrows do 60% of the acting.
- Tempo: 15% slower than her scene partners. Always.

She is not "the girl in the ad." She is the person the ad is *about*, and she happens to be looking at us.

## 10. Three scripts

### SPOT 1 — "The Guru" (:60, cuts to :30 and :15)

**Concept:** The category's default trader-guru archetype delivers his monologue. Ksenia is in the frame the whole time, unimpressed, being correct in the background.

**Setting:** A rented penthouse Airbnb pretending to be a "trading floor." One monitor. A Lambo key on the desk. Fake plants.

---

FADE IN.

**INT. "PENTHOUSE" — DAY**

BRAD (30s, gym shorts, chain, backwards cap) faces camera. Manic. Pointing.

**BRAD**
Listen. Listen to me. The market is a *war zone.* Are you a soldier or are you a *victim.*

Wide reveal: KSENIA is sitting on the couch behind him. Laptop open. Not looking up.

**BRAD (CONT'D)**
I made forty-seven thousand dollars this morning. Before my *matcha.*

**KSENIA**
(to laptop)
He lost sixty-two.

**BRAD**
(to Ksenia, still to camera)
That's — that's unrealized.

**KSENIA**
It's realized now.

Small ping from her laptop. She turns it slightly. On screen: **QUANTZY**, a clean dashboard, one green number.

**BRAD**
The market rewards *conviction.* The market rewards —

**KSENIA**
(to camera, first time)
The market rewards being right. That's the whole sentence.

She closes the laptop.

**KSENIA (CONT'D)**
Quantzy is an AI trading agent. It reads what the market is actually doing, not what Brad thinks it's doing.

Beat.

**KSENIA (CONT'D)**
Brad thinks a lot.

Cut to product UI. Clean. One number moves. Music cue in for the first time — a single, low note.

**SUPER:** *QUANTZY. Intelligence, quietly.*
**LEGAL SUPER (under her line):** *Trading involves risk. Even with us. We're honest about that.*

Cut back. Brad is still monologuing, muted. Ksenia looks at camera one more second. Doesn't smile.

CUT TO BLACK.

**End frame:** Quantzy wordmark. Below it, in smaller type: **"Quiet. Correct."**

### SPOT 2 — "The Group Chat" (:45)

**Concept:** The FOMO ad, inverted. Every crypto/betting ad shows the friend who bought early rubbing it in. We show the friend who *didn't* rub it in and just got on with her day.

**Setting:** Ksenia in a grocery store. Fluorescent light. Realistic. She's holding a lemon.

---

**INT. GROCERY STORE — DAY**

KSENIA in produce. Phone in hand. She scrolls a group chat. We see it over her shoulder in tight insert:

> **MIKE:** anyone in on $VOLT???
> **JAY:** told u
> **MIKE:** DUDE
> **JAY:** told u bro
> **MIKE:** how did u KNOW
> **JAY:** vibes

Ksenia locks the phone. Puts the lemon in her basket. Keeps walking.

**KSENIA (V.O.)**
Every group chat has a Jay. Jay does not know how he knew. Jay will not know next time either.

She stops at the eggs. Opens the carton. Checks. Closes it. Puts it in the basket.

**KSENIA (V.O.) (CONT'D)**
I have an agent that knew. It's called Quantzy. It reads the market the way I read an egg carton.

Insert of her phone. Quantzy app. Trade filled at 09:41:12. **+$1,340.**

**KSENIA (V.O.) (CONT'D)**
Before Mike asked. Before Jay lied.

She reaches checkout. The cashier is Jay from the group chat. (Same actor. Same energy. Now in an apron.)

**JAY**
Hey! Did you see —

**KSENIA**
I did.

She taps her phone. Pays. Walks out.

**SUPER:** *QUANTZY. Your agent got there first.*
**LEGAL SUPER:** *Trading involves risk. Group chats involve more.*

CUT TO BLACK.

### SPOT 3 — "The Interview" (:60)

**Concept:** A parody of the earnest fintech founder interview. Ksenia is being interviewed about Quantzy. She refuses to do the format.

**Setting:** A very obvious "podcast setup." Two mics on a wooden table. Neon "ON AIR" sign that's a little crooked. A HOST (Male, 40s, hoodie under blazer) is doing his best.

---

**INT. PODCAST STUDIO — DAY**

**HOST**
So, Ksenia. What is Quantzy — in one sentence.

**KSENIA**
It's an AI that trades for you.

Beat. Host waits for more. She doesn't give more.

**HOST**
That's — that's the sentence?

**KSENIA**
You said one.

**HOST**
Right. Okay. Um. What's the vision.

**KSENIA**
People make bad decisions with money. This one makes fewer.

**HOST**
And — and *why now?*

**KSENIA**
Because the last five years of retail trading have been, in a technical sense, embarrassing.

Small pause. Host laughs, uncertain. She doesn't.

**HOST**
Ha. Okay. Ha. Um. Are we in a bubble?

**KSENIA**
Yes.

**HOST**
Which one.

**KSENIA**
Pick one.

Cut to a montage — 2 seconds each — of screen recordings: a TikTok guy screaming, a Discord scrolling, a betting parlay slip, a rocket emoji, a rug pull tweet. Overlaid, one at a time, the specific words we don't use: **MOON. ALPHA. EDGE. DEGEN. WAGMI.** Each word gets crossed out in a single line.

Back to the interview. Silence.

**KSENIA (CONT'D)**
Quantzy doesn't use any of those words. It uses the data. It uses the model. It uses the time you were going to spend on Discord.

**HOST**
(genuinely)
That's… kind of beautiful.

**KSENIA**
It's kind of the minimum.

Beat.

**KSENIA (CONT'D)**
(to camera, not to host)
Quantzy. Intelligence and edge. For people who are done pretending.

Product UI. One number. Music cue.

**SUPER:** *QUANTZY. Done pretending.*
**LEGAL SUPER:** *Trading involves risk. We involve less of it.*

Cut back to the studio. The host is still nodding. Ksenia is already taking off her mic.

CUT TO BLACK.

## 11. What to test in the room

Before we shoot, do a table read of all three with Ksenia at **her natural tempo, not the writer's**. If a line stops feeling like her, cut it, don't rewrite it. Silence is on brand.

Success criteria for each spot in early screen tests:
1. Unaided recall of the brand name at 24 hours ≥ 40%.
2. Correct product category ("trading" or "investing") ≥ 70%.
3. Volunteered tone words in exit interviews cluster on: *dry, smart, different, adult, quiet, honest.* If we hear *funny, wacky, edgy, savage* — we've drifted into competitor territory. Recut.
